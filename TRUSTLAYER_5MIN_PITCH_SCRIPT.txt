[0:00 - 0:30] SLIDE 1: THE HOOK (PROBLEM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Spoken Script:
"Good afternoon, Judges! I’m Rishabh from Team AaluSamosa, and today we’re
presenting TrustLayer.

When travelers book stays online, platform trust breaks in two hidden ways:
First, stolen property photos get copied across listings without proof of
ownership. Legacy platforms react by slapping host bans or accusing people of fraud.
Second, silent price drift—what’s quoted on the listing page and confirmed in
the booking engine silently diverges from what the payment gateway actually charges.

This costs platforms millions in chargebacks and destroys host trust."


[0:30 - 1:15] SLIDE 2 & 3: THE SOLUTION & ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Spoken Script:
"We built TrustLayer—an independent audit and reconciliation stage sitting
beneath rental platforms like Wayzyy, OYO Rooms, and MakeMyTrip.

TrustLayer rests on three core innovations:
1. Categorical Evidence Engine: Uses perceptual hashing (dHash) to catch duplicate
   photos instantly—labeling them 'Similar media detected', NEVER making false
   'fraud' accusations.
2. Decoupled Multi-Source Reconciliation: Our Booking Service and Payment Webhook
   are 100% separate modules. If a ₹10,000 quote differs from a ₹12,000 payment
   webhook, it flags the discrepancy automatically.
3. Polygon Blockchain Witness: We anchor periodic Merkle batch root hashes to
   Polygon Amoy testnet as an unalterable timestamp witness.

Let me show you this working live in our software right now."

================================================================================
QUICK REFERENCE FOR JUDGES' Q&A
================================================================================
Q: "Does the blockchain prove ₹10,000 is the correct rent price?"
A: "No. Blockchain is strictly an immutable timestamp witness proving our database
    hash chain existed at that exact second. Amount correctness is proved by our
    Reconciliation Engine."

Q: "Why anchor a batch root hash instead of every event on-chain?"
A: "Anchoring every event is slow, expensive, and unscalable. Anchoring a periodic
    Merkle batch root hash provides 100% cryptographic tamper-evidence with a
    single lightweight transaction."

Q: "Are Booking Service and Payment Webhook really separate?"
A: "Yes, they are decoupled modules with zero shared imports or shared memory,
    communicating exclusively through append-only log records."
